## Overview

Weft is a time tracker for freelancers who bill by the hour. It turns tracked time into typeset PDF invoices. It runs in the browser and works offline, with no account: everything stays on the user's own device.

## Features

- Start and stop a timer with one key, or drag time onto the week
- Invoice a client's unbilled time with rates by client, project or task
- PDF invoices with a payment QR code and your own Stripe or PayPal link
- XRechnung and ZUGFeRD e-invoices for German clients
- Imports Harvest, Toggl Track and Clockify exports, checked to the cent before importing
- Full export and backups as plain JSON

## Pricing

- **Free**: time tracking, forever
- **One-time licence**: $69 / €69 for invoicing after a 30-day trial, with 12 months of updates

## Use Cases

- Freelancers and consultants who bill by the hour
- People moving off Harvest or Toggl who want to stop paying monthly
- Anyone who wants client and time data kept off third-party servers
