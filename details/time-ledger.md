## Overview

time-ledger is natural-language time tracking that runs as a Claude skill, with a ChatGPT custom GPT option. You describe your day in plain language, such as "read papers 2h, gym 1h", and the AI parses it into activity, duration and date rows in your own Notion database.

## Features

- Conversational logging: one sentence in a chat becomes structured rows (activity, minutes, date, tag)
- Uncertain entries are stored as "to-confirm" rows and asked about later, instead of the AI guessing durations, dates or categories
- Finds your Notion database by title, with no IDs to paste
- Rows live in your own Notion workspace (free template included) and can be exported at any time
- English and Chinese skill files, README and Notion templates

## Pricing

Free and open source (MIT). The AI input path needs a paid Claude or ChatGPT plan where skills or connectors are available; the Notion template also works on free Notion as a manual ledger.
