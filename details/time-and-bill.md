## Overview

Time & Bill is a privacy-conscious time tracking application for professionals and teams. It combines web and mobile workflows for tracking project and task time, running stopwatches, reviewing dashboards and keeping daily work records organized.

## Features

- Track work time against structured projects and tasks
- Web app plus iOS and Android mobile apps ([mobile docs](https://www.timeandbill.de/en/docs/mobile/))
- Stopwatches, plus manual creation and editing of time entries
- Reports and dashboards for tracked work and team or project information
- Managed accounts for team workflows
- Built in Germany with a focus on privacy

## Pricing

Subscription-based hosted service.
