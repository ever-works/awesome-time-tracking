## Overview

Ripple is a free app for limiting distracting apps on Android and iPhone. It is written for two groups: adults who want limits on their own phone, and parents who set limits on a child's phone (on that phone itself; there is no remote management).

## Features

- Usage calendar on Android: total time per day, plus time, opens and blocks for each app, kept on the phone for 365 days
- Schedules that block chosen apps during set hours, on Android and iPhone
- Daily time limits per app on Android, including a 0 minute limit that keeps an app closed all day
- Open limits and a short pause before an app opens on Android
- Quick Focus on Android: lock distracting apps right away for a set number of minutes
- Strict mode on Android: rules can only be relaxed with an unlock code you can give to a friend or family member, with Anti-Uninstall turned on

## Platforms

- Android: APK download from the website or GitHub Releases (not in Google Play)
- iPhone (iOS 16+): schedule blocking, Anti-Uninstall, clock change protection and unlock codes only

## Pricing

Free, with no ads and no account. Settings and usage records stay on the phone.
