# Front of Mind

**Category:** Desktop Time Tracking Applications  
**Tags:** desktop, macos, windows, integration

[Visit Website](https://keepfrontofmind.com)

---

## Description
Front of Mind is a floating time tracker for Mac and Windows. Pick a task from Linear, Asana or monday.com, press play, and the time is written back to the task: a time card in Linear, a comment in Asana, an update in monday.com. It only times what you start, with no screenshots or activity monitoring.

---

## Features
- Small floating timer that stays on screen
- Task picker for Linear, Asana and monday.com
- Time written back to each task
- Manual time entries and session notes
- Pauses when you step away from the computer
- Personal reports with CSV export
- Team reports on the Team plan
