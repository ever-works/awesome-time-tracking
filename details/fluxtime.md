## Features

- Journal-style time log: add an entry when you switch tasks, and each entry runs until the next one, with no timer to start or stop.
- Day planner with tasks, events and reminders on a calendar timeline, plus AI suggestions for what to do next.
- Goals broken into projects and todos, with progress measured in hours actually logged.
- Reviews of time by category against the previous period, an energy curve across the day and goal pace forecasts.
- Quick Log on Mac, templates on Apple Watch, widgets and Apple Calendar integration.
- Remote MCP server so Claude and other MCP clients can read the plan and time log.

## Pricing

Free to download on the App Store, with an optional Flux Pro subscription for cloud sync, deeper analytics and the MCP server.
